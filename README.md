# Sentio — Feedback & Issue Operations Platform

> **Survey App: Rating and Feedback System Application**

Sentio is a modern feedback management platform designed to help organizations collect, understand, and act on customer, employee, event, product, and service feedback.

Unlike traditional survey applications that stop after collecting responses, Sentio connects the complete feedback lifecycle:

**Collect → Understand → Identify Issues → Take Action → Measure Improvement**

---

## Overview

Sentio provides a centralized platform for creating surveys, collecting responses, analyzing feedback, identifying recurring issues, and tracking improvement actions.

The platform is designed to be flexible enough for different industries and use cases, including:

- Business and customer feedback
- Employee feedback
- Restaurant and hospitality reviews
- Event and conference evaluations
- Retail and e-commerce feedback
- Healthcare and wellness feedback
- Fitness and recreation feedback
- Academic and course evaluations
- Product and SaaS feedback
- Professional services

---

## Key Features

### Survey Builder

Create customized surveys using multiple question types:

- Star Rating
- Numeric Rating
- Emoji / Mood Rating
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Likert Scale
- Multiple Choice
- Checkbox
- Yes / No
- Short Text
- Long Text
- Dropdown

Survey creators can:

- Add and edit questions
- Reorder questions
- Duplicate questions
- Mark questions as required
- Add categories
- Configure rating ranges
- Add answer options
- Preview surveys
- Save surveys as drafts
- Publish surveys

---

### Survey Templates

Sentio includes ready-to-use templates for different scenarios, including:

- Customer Experience
- Employee Engagement
- Event & Conference Feedback
- Academic Course Evaluation
- Healthcare & Clinic Feedback
- Fitness Facility Feedback
- Post-Purchase & Delivery Feedback

Templates can be used as starting points and customized for individual requirements.

---

### Public Survey Experience

Published surveys can be shared with respondents through a public survey link and QR code.

The public survey experience supports:

- Mobile-friendly layouts
- Multiple question types
- Required-field validation
- Anonymous responses
- Progress tracking
- Conditional questions
- Rating interactions
- Response confirmation

---

### QR Code Sharing

Each published survey can be shared using a QR code.

The sharing interface provides options to:

- Display the QR code
- Copy the public survey link
- Download the QR code
- Share the survey

The public survey is designed to work across desktop and mobile devices.

---

### Feedback Intelligence

Sentio analyzes written feedback using a lightweight client-side intelligence system.

The system can identify:

- Positive sentiment
- Neutral sentiment
- Negative sentiment
- Recurring topics
- Potential problem areas

Detected topics can include areas such as:

- Waiting Time & Speed
- Service & Staff Attentiveness
- Quality & Freshness
- Product & Workflow Experience
- Pricing & Value
- Facilities & Cleanliness

---

### Feedback Pulse

Feedback Pulse provides a high-level view of overall feedback health.

It combines metrics such as:

- Overall feedback score
- Average rating
- Positive sentiment
- Neutral sentiment
- Negative sentiment
- Response volume
- Open issues
- Resolved issues
- Feedback trends

This allows users to quickly understand the current state of their feedback.

---

### Issue Radar

Issue Radar converts recurring negative feedback into actionable issues.

Issues can include:

- Severity
- Status
- Category
- Average rating
- Number of mentions
- Trend percentage
- Responsible owner
- Related feedback quotes

Issue statuses include:

- New
- Under Review
- In Progress
- Action Taken
- Resolved
- Reopened

---

### Action Tracking

Each issue can contain action items assigned to responsible users.

Actions can be:

- Created
- Assigned
- Updated
- Completed
- Tracked through issue history

This creates a connection between feedback and operational improvements.

---
